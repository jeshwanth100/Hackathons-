# SchemeSetu: Sovereign Civic-FinTech & Financial Inclusion Platform

SchemeSetu is an AI-powered financial inclusion platform designed to eliminate bureaucratic friction between micro-entrepreneurs and sovereign capital across India.

Imported directly from **Google Stitch** (`projects/1077539319220458027`).

---

## 🏛️ Platform Highlights

* **Smart Capital & Eligibility Engine**: Real-time evaluation against flagship government schemes (PMEGP, Mudra Tarun/Kishor/Shishu, Stand-Up India, PM SVANidhi).
* **Dynamic Loan, EMI & Subsidy Calculator**: Interactive simulation with upfront capital grant deduction (up to 35% under PMEGP) and tenure selection.
* **Setu Saathi AI Copilot**: Proactive AI touchpoint demystifying scheme criteria, collateral-free credit under CGTMSE, and document checklists with audio-guided assistance.
* **Sovereign Bento-Grid Design**: Built on modern Tier-1 FinTech aesthetics combined with official sovereign public trust badges and WCAG AAA compliance.
